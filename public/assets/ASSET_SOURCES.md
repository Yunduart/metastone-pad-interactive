# Asset sources

- `metastone-domain-map-source.png` — user-supplied visual/source frame for the field-film demo slot.
- `shishi-logo.svg` — existing METASTONE brand asset from the project workspace.
- `earth-atmos-2048.jpg` — Three.js official planet example texture.
- `earth-blue-2048.jpg` — blue-white duotone derivative of `earth-atmos-2048.jpg`, prepared locally to match the selected visual's luminous planetary treatment.
- `earth-normal-2048.jpg` — Three.js official planet example texture.
- `earth-clouds-1024.png` — Three.js official planet example texture.
- `orbital-space-background.png` — generated from the selected visual's art direction as a clean 3:2 background plate; all UI, planets, paths, and typography were removed and the star field was reconstructed.

The Three.js example textures were retrieved from the official `mrdoob/three.js` repository and are used under the repository's MIT license:

`https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets`
